[I recently blogged](/blog/the-problem-the-making-of-me-chschmid-com-part-1/ "The Making of me.chschmid.com Part 1: The Problem") about why it makes sense to run your own server. Although I have a machine running at me.chschmid.com already, I decided to do a serious hardware upgrade.

![server](./the-making-of-me-chschmid-com-part-2-the-hardware/server.jpg)Not because the old AMD E-350 CPU can't keep up anymore, but because I'd like to use the new machine as my desktop PC as well. So here's what I ordered (some parts of it are actually not new, but carry over from my old setup)

-   A nice package: [Fractal Design DEFINE R4 Black Pearl](http://www.fractal-design.com/home/product/cases/define-series/define-r4-black-pearl)
-   Power: [be quite! System Power 7 | 300W](http://www.bequiet.com/de/powersupply/325)
-   The base: [Gigabyte GA-Z87-HD3](http://www.gigabyte.com/products/product-page.aspx?pid=4489#ov)
-   Some short term memory: [8GB Dual Channel DDR3 Memory Kit](http://www.corsair.com/en/memory-by-product-family/vengeance/vengeance-8gb-dual-channel-ddr3-memory-kit-cmz8gx3m2a1600c9g.html)
-   Some long term memory: [Samsung 250GB 2.5-inch 840 EVO SSD](http://www.samsung.com/uk/consumer/memory-cards-hdd-odd/ssd/840-evo/MZ-7TE250BW)
-   Some more long term memory: [Western Digital Red 3TB](http://www.wdc.com/en/products/products.aspx?id=810)
-   The powerhorse: [Intel Xeon E3-1245V3](http://cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=Intel+Xeon+E3-1245+v3+%40+3.40GHz)

For all I know this system will be hopelessly over-powered, yet power-efficient, so that it can run 24/7. The parts are probably going to arrive by the end of the week. I'll keep you posted about the assembly and everything else that's going to happen.
