I started doing reharmonizations of Christmas tunes for brass quartet (2 trumpets, trombone or baritone, tuba or bass trombone) a couple of years ago. For convenience I've decided to publish some of them in one book: This is [Christmas Reharmonized, Volume 1](/files/2019/11/Christmas-Reharmonized-Volume-1.zip). It includes five tunes:

1 - [Andachtsjodler](/blog/andachtsjodler-merry-christmas-from-austria/) - an Austrian Christmas Carol

<audio controls="" src="/files/2016/11/Andachtsjodler-2019-Take-2.mp3"></audio>

2 - [Alle Jahre Wieder](/blog/alle-jahre-wieder-merry-christmas/) - a German Christmas Carol

<audio controls="" src="/files/2019/10/Piano-Alle-Jahre-wieder.mp3"></audio>

3 - [Leise Rieselt der Schnee](/blog/leise-rieselt-der-schnee-merry-christmas-2/) - a German Christmas Carol

<audio controls="" src="/files/2019/10/Leise-Rieselt-der-Schnee-Take-2.mp3"></audio>

4 - [Es Wird Scho Glei Dumpa](/blog/es-wird-scho-glei-dumpa-merry-christmas-from-austria/) - an Austrian Christmas Carol

<audio controls="" src="/files/2018/12/Piano-Es-wird-scho-glei-dumpa-Take-2019.mp3"></audio>

5 - [Adeste Fideles](/blog/adeste-fideles-o-come-all-ye-faithful/) - O Come, All Ye Faithful

<audio controls="" src="/files/2019/11/Adeste-Fideles-Take-1.mp3"></audio>

All of the arrangements consist of parts using traditional arranging techniques and of reharmonized parts.

The [download](/files/2019/11/Christmas-Reharmonized-Volume-1.zip) includes printable sheet music (pdf) and audio files (mp3) of the tunes played on piano. All of this under a considerate [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)!

Merry Christmas!
