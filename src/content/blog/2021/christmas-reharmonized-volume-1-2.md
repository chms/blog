I started doing reharmonizations of Christmas tunes for brass quartet (2 trumpets, trombone or baritone, tuba or bass trombone) a couple of years ago. For your convenience I've compiled them all in one book: This is [Christmas Reharmonized, Volume 1 & 2 (FIXME: FILE TOO BIG)](https://blog.chschmid.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Christmas-Reharmonized-Volume-1-2.zip). It includes eight tunes:
