Send your personal message out into world via [Morse code](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_code) transmitted by my Philips Hue light bulbs. If you want to see what people send, you might want to stand [here](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=48.33433,14.312107&ll=48.335171,14.312927&spn=0.004141,0.007907&num=1&t=h&z=18) or [here](https://maps.google.com/maps?q=48.334854,14.311397&num=1&t=h&z=18).

This is the next evolutionary step from a previous project called [PIMORSE](/blog/pilight/). If you would like to take a look at the source code head over to [https://github.com/chms/huemorse](https://github.com/chms/huemorse).
